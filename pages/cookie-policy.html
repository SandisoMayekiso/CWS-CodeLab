Cookie Policy

This page explains how CWS CodeLab may use cookies, local storage and similar browser technologies and how optional technologies should be controlled.

Last updated: 23 August 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by your browser when you visit a website. Websites may also use technologies such as local storage, session storage and similar browser mechanisms for functional purposes.

2. CodeLab cookie categories

Category Purpose Default
Essential Authentication, security, navigation, account sessions and saved privacy preferences. Required
Analytics Optional measurement of how CodeLab is used so the platform can be improved. Off until allowed
Embedded Media Third-party lesson videos, interactive media or external learning content. Off until allowed
Marketing Optional advertising, campaign measurement or remarketing if introduced in the future. Off until allowed

3. Essential technologies

Some browser storage may be required for security, authentication, account sessions, navigation or privacy preferences.

These technologies may be necessary for the learning platform to function correctly.

4. Analytics

CWS CodeLab may introduce analytics tools to understand general platform usage, identify technical problems and improve learning experiences.

Optional analytics should not be activated before the required preference or consent mechanism has been implemented where applicable.

5. Embedded learning media

Future CodeLab lessons may include external media such as video lessons or interactive resources.

Where practical, privacy-enhanced embedding and appropriate consent controls should be used before third-party media technologies are loaded.

6. Local storage

CodeLab may use browser local storage for interface preferences or other limited browser-side functionality.

Browser storage should not be treated as a secure substitute for server-side authorisation, subscription checks, assessment security or protected student records.

7. Your choices

When optional cookies or technologies are introduced, CodeLab intends to provide controls allowing users to choose whether optional categories can be used.

Cookie preference controls are not yet active.

The preference interface will be introduced when CodeLab begins using optional analytics, embedded-media or marketing technologies that require these controls.

8. Browser controls

Your browser may allow you to block, remove or inspect cookies and local storage. Blocking essential browser storage may prevent some CodeLab features from functioning correctly.

9. Changing this policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated as CodeLab introduces authentication, analytics, video lessons, payment services, advertising or other platform functionality.

10. Privacy

For more information about how CodeLab may process personal information, read the Privacy Policy .
