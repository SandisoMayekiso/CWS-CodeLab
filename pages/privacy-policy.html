Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CWS CodeLab may collect, use, store and protect personal information when you use the public website or learning platform.

Last updated: 23 August 2026

1. Information we may process

CWS CodeLab may process information such as your name, email address, account information, authentication information, course enrolment, lesson progress, project progress, assessment results, subscription or entitlement status, certificate records, support communications and technical information required to operate and secure the platform.

2. Why we process information

Information may be used to create and manage CodeLab accounts, provide learning content, save course progress, administer assessments, manage projects, provide eligible certificates, manage subscriptions, provide support, maintain platform security and improve CWS CodeLab.

3. Account and authentication services

CWS CodeLab may use external technology providers to support authentication, database storage, hosting, payments, analytics, video delivery and other platform services.

When these providers are introduced or changed, their own privacy policies and data-handling practices may also apply.

4. Learning records

CodeLab may maintain records associated with your learning activity, including courses, completed lessons, assessments, projects, learning paths and progress.

These records are intended to support the student learning experience and should not be publicly exposed unless a specific feature, such as public credential verification, requires limited information to be displayed.

5. Certificates and credential verification

Eligible CWS CodeLab certificates may eventually include a public verification record so an employer, recruiter or other third party can confirm whether a credential is valid.

Public verification should expose only the information reasonably necessary to verify the credential and should not expose private student learning records.

6. Cookies and browser storage

CWS CodeLab may use cookies, local storage or similar browser technologies for essential platform functionality, authentication, preferences and security.

Optional technologies such as analytics, embedded media or marketing tools should be subject to appropriate user controls when introduced.

Read the Cookie Policy for more information.

7. Payments and subscriptions

If paid CodeLab subscriptions are introduced, payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider.

CWS CodeLab should not store complete payment card details directly when payment processing is handled by an authorised external payment provider.

8. Security

CWS CodeLab aims to use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect platform information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, but access controls, authentication protections and secure backend rules should be used where appropriate.

9. Data retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate accounts, preserve learning records, provide credentials, maintain security, resolve support requests or meet applicable legal and operational requirements.

10. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, users may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, consent withdrawal and complaints concerning their personal information.

11. Children and student accounts

Additional safeguards may be required where CodeLab services are offered to children or younger learners.

Age requirements and any required parental or guardian consent should be communicated before such accounts or services are introduced.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as CWS CodeLab introduces additional functionality, including authentication services, payments, analytics, video delivery, certificates, subscriptions or additional learning tools.

The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.
