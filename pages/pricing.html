Pricing FAQ

Common questions

Can I start without paying?

Yes. CodeLab Free lets you begin learning developer foundations before purchasing a subscription.

What happens when I upgrade?

Your existing account and learning progress remain available while eligible Pro courses and features are unlocked.

Are certificates included?

Eligible CodeLab certificates are planned as part of the Pro learning experience after completion requirements are met.

Is Pro required for every course?

No. Several developer foundation courses remain available through CodeLab Free, while advanced courses can require Pro.

How much will CodeLab Pro cost?

The final subscription price has not yet been published. Pricing will be displayed here before paid subscriptions are enabled.

Is CWS Academy included?

CWS Academy and CWS CodeLab belong to the CyberWithSandiso learning ecosystem, but they are separate learning schools and their access can be managed separately.
