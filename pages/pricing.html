Questions before you start

Clear answers, without fine-print surprises

If a commercial term has not been finalized, this page says so directly.

Can I start without paying?+

Yes. You can create an account and use courses marked Free without entering payment details.

Can I buy Pro now?+

No. Pro is a preview while the paid offering, billing terms and support process are being finalized.

Will every course issue a certificate?+

No. Certificate eligibility depends on the specific course and its published completion requirements. Finishing a lesson alone does not guarantee a certificate.

What happens to my free progress?+

Your course progress is tied to your account. Access still depends on the course status and the platform's published terms.

Where are cancellation and refund terms?+

No paid checkout is active. Those terms must be added and accepted before any Pro subscription is sold.
