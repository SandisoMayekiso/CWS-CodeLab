Pricing FAQ

Common questions

Can I start without paying?

Yes. CodeLab Free is designed to let students begin learning developer fundamentals before purchasing a subscription.

What happens when I upgrade?

Your account will retain your existing progress while Pro features and eligible courses are unlocked.

Are certificates included?

Eligible CodeLab certificates are planned as part of the Pro learning experience and will depend on completing the required course criteria.

Is Pro required for every course?

No. Several foundation courses remain available through the Free plan while advanced courses and features can require Pro access.

Can pricing change?

Subscription pricing and billing details will be published before paid CodeLab subscriptions are enabled.

Is CWS Academy included?

CWS Academy and CWS CodeLab are connected learning platforms, but their course and subscription access can be managed separately.
